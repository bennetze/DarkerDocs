DarkerDocs · Local to this browser

A page looks different?

After updating DarkerDocs, reload your open Workspace pages once. Choose Default in the popup to restore Google’s original appearance.

Slides artwork and images always stay original. A preservation fallback also keeps an affected surface original when it cannot be safely themed. Repeated rendering limits may return after a reload.

Theme changes follow your system’s reduced-motion preference.

Waiting for supported Workspace pages…

Only already-scoped Docs, Sheets, Slides and Forms pages respond. No document text, file URLs, tab titles or unrelated tabs are included.
